## Preface

The story all started with this shity little boombox, it's a $10 I bought with my wife from Goodwill. The FM and CD player helps us replaying my father-in-law's CD collection gift (in a carry on suitcase). The convienient is good, but sound is really shit. So I start to think of replace those two speakers using parts from Parts Express.

1. either swap the speaker, the box plastic case has little to modified.
2. or make it a front-end source ( has no analogy output, and so difficult to extract the digital or analog signal from the board), then connect to a good DIY speaker.

Eventually I found this Panasonic SC-PM39D on shopgoodwill.com for $43 include shipping. Features, cassette, CD, DVD, with a Optical Digital Output.

## Sonab OD-11
There is bunch of recourses were maintained by [CarlssonPlanet](https://carlssonplanet.com/en/speakers/produced/sonab-od-11/) or Carlsson foundation. The outer looks it's easy to design, but not the internal parts (even we can find some internal structure pictures from [here](https://josefsson.org/od11/) and [there](https://www.carlssonkult.se/OD-11Cloud.aspx/)). There was a pair of TE OD-11 (for parts, ~$500 each) sell on eBay this May, but very little was traded in the US but many are selling on its parent country platform [Blocket](https://www.blocket.se/). Consider the cost and shipment from Sweden, it's still not a good idea to ship them all the way from Sweden.

Eventually, I was so lucky to found this pair from eBay this June a pair for $260. The disassembled parts helps me a lot in the final design of this opensource project.
